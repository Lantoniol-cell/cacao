Dictionary of variables

The 328 columns of the dataset, one per row. Search by name or description, filter, sort by clicking a header, and click a row to open its full sheet.

Each sheet is built by scripts/45_dictionnaire_qualite.py from the collection code. Periods, completeness and checks are computed on the actual data. Anything not documented in the code is marked “Not documented”. The texts of the sheets were written in French and translated for this page; variable names are unchanged.

NoteHow to read “interest for forecasting”

No horizon is assigned by hand. The sheet reports three different measures, none of which proves causality:

  1. SHAP rank: the best rank of the variables derived from this column in the model, by target and horizon (for example “direction 20: 12/142”). It says what the model relies on, not whether it improves the forecast.
  2. Association: the rank correlations retained with the future target, in the 2010-2026 sample, after multiple-testing correction.
  3. Loss when the family is removed, out of sample. A positive value with an asterisk means that the family clearly helps the model.

In every case, no factor model beats the benchmark without factors: predictive interest remains a hypothesis to test.
